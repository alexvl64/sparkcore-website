Strategy Overview

CryptoVision is SparkCore's active, defensive strategy. The fund combines an actively managed directional sleeve with a market-neutral core to participate in the potential of digital assets without taking permanent passive exposure.Since its strategy overhaul in March 2025, CryptoVision targets more durable and resilient exposure to digital assets, with a maximum drawdown target of 15%. The allocation between the two blocks is adjusted dynamically according to market conditions, volatility, liquidity and the risk/return profile. The portfolio is rebalanced monthly within a strict risk-management framework.

Defensive block — Primary core
Market-neutral & defensive algorithms
This block brings together market-neutral strategies and defensive algorithmic approaches. Its role is to ensure the structural stability of the portfolio, including during periods of high volatility or market stress. Active management of exposure, liquidity and drawdown is central, with algorithmic execution governed by strict risk parameters.
Offensive block — Opportunistic sleeve
Managed directional exposure
This sleeve actively invests in the most liquid digital assets, primarily Bitcoin and Ethereum. It aims to capture favourable market phases when trend, momentum and risk signals justify it. Exposure may be reduced when visibility decreases, volatility rises or the risk/return profile becomes less favourable. Short positions may be initiated on reversal signals, and moderate leverage may be used within a defined risk framework.
Overall strategic balance — CryptoVision is built on a flexible allocation between a primary defensive core and a managed directional sleeve.The defensive block seeks to stabilise the fund's profile, while the offensive block enables participation in favourable market phases. The weighting between the blocks evolves over time to preserve consistency between targeted performance, accepted volatility and drawdown control.
Risk profile — CryptoVision is designed for investors seeking active, structured and defensive exposure to digital assets.The fund positions itself between an offensive directional strategy and a market-neutral strategy. It accepts a degree of directional exposure, but this remains managed within a defined risk framework, with particular attention paid to volatility, the liquidity of the instruments used and the portfolio's behaviour in market-stress conditions.
