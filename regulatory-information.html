SparkCore manages alternative investment funds structured as Estonian limited partnerships (usaldusfond): Dynamic Trends, CryptoVision and Equinoxe. SparkCore.investment OÜ acts as general partner of the funds.

Our funds are marketed to professional investors. Other investors may, subject to conditions, access them within the limits of the Estonian regime. The minimum investment is €50,000, in EUR, USD or regulated stablecoins.
