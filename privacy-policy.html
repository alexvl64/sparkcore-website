3. Investors

If you invest in a fund managed by SparkCore, we process the data needed to identify you and to manage your investment: identity and contact details, identification documents, source-of-funds information, tax residence, subscription and redemption documents, and your holdings. Anti-money-laundering checks are a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR; Estonian Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Prevention Act); managing your investment is necessary for the performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).

Subscription documents are signed on an electronic signature platform hosted on our own servers. Your investor portal account (investor.sparkcore-investment.com) uses your email address and, if you enable it, a hardware security key; it gives you access to your positions, reports and documents.

4. Recipients and processors

Your data is accessible only to authorised SparkCore staff and to the service providers below, acting on our instructions:

  • Cloudflare: website hosting and delivery, security (Turnstile anti-bot check), encrypted backups.
  • OVHcloud: servers hosting our internal applications and databases, in the OVHcloud data centre in Erith (London, United Kingdom).
  • Mailjet: sending emails and the newsletter.
  • Telegram: internal notification of new contact requests to our team (name, email, phone number).
  • Cal.com: call booking.
  • Google (Google Analytics 4): audience measurement, only with your consent.
  • Cronitor: website performance monitoring, without identifying data.
  • jsDelivr: delivery of open-source scripts used by the website (receives your IP address).

Our accounting firm, legal counsel, banks and the competent authorities may receive investor data where required by law or by the management of the funds.

5. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Our servers are located in the United Kingdom, which benefits from an adequacy decision of the European Commission. Some providers may also process data in other countries outside the EEA; such transfers rely on an adequacy decision (including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for certified providers) or on the Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Cookies and local storage

Audience measurement cookies are only set after you accept them in the consent banner (Google Consent Mode v2, refused by default). The website also stores two technical preferences in your browser: your language choice and your consent choice. These do not identify you.

8. Your rights

You may request access to your data, its rectification or erasure, the restriction of its processing, its portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. You may withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out before. Some data cannot be erased while a legal retention obligation applies.

To exercise your rights, write to contact@sparkcore.fund. We reply within one month.

9. Complaints

You may lodge a complaint with the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon) or with the data protection authority of your country of residence in the EU.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS). Access to our internal applications is restricted to authorised staff with strong authentication, and investor documents are stored encrypted.

11. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or in the law. The date of the latest version is shown at the top of this page.
